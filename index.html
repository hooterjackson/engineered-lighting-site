The idea

01

It sees the room.

Cameras tell it where each person is and what they are doing, not who they are. People are A and B; naming someone is their choice.

02

It decides on light for each person.

A bright pool for someone chopping vegetables, a soft, steady glow for someone reading: at the same time, without one bothering the other, and with the room still looking good.

03

It checks, and it learns.

It checks that the light landed where it was meant to, and learns from the changes people make.
