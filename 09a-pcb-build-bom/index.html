    Buy the parts for the PCB build¶

    Two complete fixtures · LIGHT v0.2 / cbb8d9fc · reviewed 13 September 2026

    Check a required line when its full quantity for both fixtures is covered by an order, usable stock you will have in Canada, or an equivalent included part. If you choose an alternative below, also check the required line it replaces; that line will then leave the remaining-shopping list. Your checks save in this browser, independently of the earlier bench checklist. They do not sync between your phone and computer.

    Selection notes matter: “Specified” identifies the required part; it does not certify supplier stock or delivery. Rows saying “Select” or “Confirm” still need that decision before purchase. DigiKey Canada product links were checked on 13 September 2026 where an exact match or clearly labelled candidate was found. Prices, stock and delivery dates can change; verify them in the cart. A Canadian storefront does not mean the parts ship from a Canadian warehouse. Printed gimbal structures and diffuser supports are excluded.

    Order from fewer suppliers: DigiKey links below cover the cable-side connectors, shorter PA/PH leads, Carclo optic, mounting tapes and selected assembly supplies. Keep the specialist sources for the manufactured PCBs, VALENT X tape, motors, custom spotlight star/housing, diffuser and aluminum tube. PSU/input hardware, moving-wire specification and dimension-dependent fasteners still need selection. Do not add the already-fitted main-PCB components to your order.

    What the adversarial review changed¶

    • Corrected the tape arithmetic: actual 2.46″ cut increments give 11.48′ for two fixtures with the provisional ring allowance. Ring geometry still needs a dry-fit.
    • Recovered the actual spotlight configuration and housing: the bench uses warm-white XP-E2, cool-white XP-E2 and PC Amber; the documented housing is DLH-3UP-EH. A generic three-LED star may have the wrong internal connections.
    • Added two omitted mounting adhesives: 1.1 mm VHB 4611 under the flex bands and 0.05 mm 467MP for the static gimbal flex. These are user-applied; they are not the factory PI stiffeners.
    • Corrected the Yupo identifier: the maker lists L21-YPT153WH1114; the earlier Amazon title used YUP.
    • Reduced loose-crimp purchasing: exact PA and PH pre-crimp lead options are listed. GH wire compatibility and moving-loop lengths remain unresolved.
    • Separated mains integration from the old bench BOM: the PSU, socket input, insulation, retention and associated hardware must be selected together. The earlier 90 W PSU and its insulation assumptions are not an automatic fit for this revision.

    The remaining selection work is concentrated in tube supplier/thermal confirmation and mounting dimensions, spotlight configuration/thermal check, PSU/input arrangement, GH wire and moving harness routing. These are open assembly decisions, not evidence that the manufactured boards have been physically validated.

    Do not buy these twice¶

    The factory PCBA includes the ESP32-C6, driver electronics, regulators, protection parts, C504 and all fitted board-side connectors. The old ESP32 dev kit, PCA9685 modules, ULN2803s, PicoBuck, Pololu regulator, protoboards, socket headers, JST-XH kit, XT30 adapters and bare bus wire belong to the earlier bench build. They are not required purchases here.

    J1 takes bare stripped power wires; J2 takes the upper flex directly. The static gimbal stations are solder connections. J18 is expansion solder pads, not another fitted connector. J16 brake/bus hardware is not required for the initial build; decide on an energy-dissipation accessory only if commissioning establishes a need. The previous frame's extra 608ZZ bearing is also excluded.
